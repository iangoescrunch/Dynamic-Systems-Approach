Chapter Overview
Introduction
The Developmental Puzzle
Orderliness at low magnification does not require prescriptive instructions. Thelen identifies the explanatory regress in maturational, learning, and interactionist accounts.
Problem framing
Chapter 1
Rethinking Development
The case against stage theory and maturational clocks. Any account that locates developmental instructions in a pre-existing structure faces infinite regress.
Falsification
Chapter 2
A Dynamic Systems Framework
Attractors, order parameters, control parameters, and phase transitions imported from physics. These become the analytic tools for developmental data.
Framework import
Chapter 3
Infant Kicking — Soft Assembly
EMG on infant legs shows co-contraction, not CPG reciprocal inhibition. The kick is a spring-mass system. Coordination emerges without neural encoding of the full pattern.
EMG · Kinematics
Chapter 4
Causality and the Developmental System
Treadmill, water immersion, and weighted cuffs triple-converge: mass/strength ratio is the control parameter for stepping disappearance. Not cortical inhibition.
Converging falsification
Chapter 5
The Embodied Mind
3D motion capture of infant reaching across longitudinal sessions. Movement units decrease as attractors deepen. Variability indexes developmental state, not noise.
Longitudinal kinematics
Chapter 6
Development as Selection
Spontaneous movement generates repertoire. The mobile-kick contingency shows selection acts on existing behavior, not instruction. Extends Edelman's Neural Darwinism to development.
Selectionist extension
Chapter 7
The Dynamics of Selection
Intermodal integration as the developmental primitive. Movement as perception. Rovee-Collier's mobile paradigm reinterpreted as attractor formation: context-bound memory, variable training, and the selectionist bridge from real-time learning to development.
Analysis & Reflection
Chapter 8
The Context-Specific Origin of Knowledge
Context makes, selects, and adapts knowledge. Adolph's slopes show posture-specific décalage. Baillargeon reinterpreted through trajectory dynamics. Novel word bifurcation from small contextual forces. Intelligence at the edge of multiple attractors.
Analysis & Reflection
Chapter 9
Knowledge from Action
Learning to reach as the full NDS empirical program. Gabriel and Hannah discover opposite solutions from opposite intrinsic dynamics. Nathan's year-long data show exploration, selection, and shifting control parameters. Action and knowledge share the same dynamics.

The project tracks how theory is built through the accumulation of empirical evidence — which accounts are falsified, which are extended, and what new territory opens under the lens of dynamic systems theory.
